Privacy Policy for Jug Up: Water Tracker

Last updated: August 2026

Jug Up ("we", "our", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it's used.

Information We Collect

Data stored only on your device: your logged drinks, goals, streaks, saved cups, and theme preferences. This data is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. If you export a backup file, that file is created and shared by you — we don't receive a copy.

Camera: used only to scan barcodes when you choose to scan a bottle. We do not store, transmit, or access your camera roll or any photos.

Location: used only to check local weather, so the app can suggest a higher hydration goal on hot days — and only if you've turned this feature on in Settings. Location is not stored or shared beyond this purpose.

Notifications: used to send hydration reminders you configure. You can disable these anytime in Settings or your device's notification settings.

Third-Party Services

Advertising (free version only): we use Google AdMob to show ads to free users. Jug Up Plus subscribers do not see ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, per Google's own privacy policy.

Subscriptions: we use RevenueCat to process Jug Up Plus subscriptions through the Google Play billing system. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

App updates: we use Expo's EAS Update service to deliver app updates, which involves your device checking Expo's servers for available updates.

Children's Privacy

Jug Up is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can disable camera, location, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings without losing core app functionality — manual water logging still works.

Data Deletion

Since your data is stored locally, uninstalling the app deletes it. If you'd like help removing any other data, contact us at the email below.
